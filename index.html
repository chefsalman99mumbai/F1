DETAILS.

Is F1 Acoustic only for luxury homes?+

No. The system thinking scales from individual rooms to premium residences, hospitality, offices, amenities and developer portfolios.

What is the difference between soundproofing and acoustic treatment?+

Soundproofing stops sound from entering or leaving a space (isolation), while acoustic treatment controls how sound behaves inside a room (absorption, diffusion). We design both as a unified system.

Can soundproofing be invisible?+

Acoustic assemblies can be coordinated into walls, ceilings, doors and junction details so performance supports rather than competes with the architecture.

Do you work with architects and project teams?+

Yes. Acoustic performance depends on coordination across design, services, material transitions and execution.

Where do you operate?+

F1 Acoustic is Mumbai-based, with the positioning and experience focused on premium architectural and real-estate projects.
