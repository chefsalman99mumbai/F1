Is F1 Acoustic only for luxury homes? +

No. The system thinking scales from a single room to premium developer portfolios, hospitality, offices and amenity floors.

That is the objective. Acoustic assemblies can be integrated into walls, ceilings, doors and junction details so acoustic performance supports the visual language.

Do you work with architects? +

Yes. Coordination is critical: acoustic intent needs to survive drawings, MEP services, material transitions and site execution.

F1 Acoustic is Mumbai-based, with a focus on premium real-estate and architectural projects.
