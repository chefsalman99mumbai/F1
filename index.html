What we do

F1 Acoustic designs and delivers soundproofing and acoustic treatment for premium spaces. We stop unwanted sound from entering or leaving a room, and we shape how sound behaves inside it — so it feels natural, private and calm.

Who we work with

Homeowners, architects, interior designers and developers. Whether it's a single bedroom, a private cinema, a work studio or an entire building standard — the approach scales.

How we work

We start by understanding the site — the structure, adjacencies and how the room will be used. Then we specify a layered system of walls, ceilings, doors, floors and details that work together, not in isolation.

What makes us different

We treat acoustics as part of the architecture, not an add-on. The result is performance you can measure, with a finish you'd want to live with — invisible where it should be, purposeful where it matters.
